# GDS-10 Cross-System Validation

> **Phase:** GDS-10 — Social Play, Cooperation, Competition, and PvP Boundaries  
> **Status:** PASS  
> **Purpose:** Validate the GDS-10 social contract against closed GDS-1 through GDS-9 authority and confirm clean downstream ownership boundaries.

## 1. Validation Scope

GDS-10 is cross-validated against:

- GDS-1 product identity, target audience, social positioning, session shape and non-loss-dominant promise;
- GDS-2 session/persistence, Finalized Outcome, Recovery and Protected Load Failure rules;
- GDS-3 movement, semantic controls, onboarding, Active Context and accessibility obligations;
- GDS-4 one-owner secured creature model, Creature Lock, Release, discovery and provenance;
- GDS-5 Engagement Claim, capture, Transport Custody, interruption and exact single-winner finalization;
- GDS-6 rarity, Variant identity, discovery, Protected Variant and probability-integrity rules;
- GDS-7 Vault ownership, Visitor read-only authority, production and capacity rules;
- GDS-8 Energy non-transferability, active rewards, Milestones and transaction integrity;
- GDS-9 world access, Region Mastery, public encounter, travel, hazard and rare-encounter rules;
- GDS-11 through GDS-16 authority boundaries;
- Technical Architecture gating.

## 2. GDS-1 Product Compatibility

### Social creature-collection identity

Parties, Shared Objectives, public pursuit, Showcases, Vault visits and Friendly Challenges create reasons to notice and intentionally play with other people.

**PASS.**

### Non-loss-dominant positioning

GDS-10 explicitly prohibits ordinary direct-combat PvP, player-caused secured-creature theft, Transport Custody seizure, Energy theft, wagering and involuntary persistent-value loss.

**PASS.**

### Core loop remains available solo

No Party, friend relation, challenge, visitor action, unrestricted chat or voice is required to explore, capture, secure, manage the Vault or progress through ordinary regions.

**PASS.**

### Social competition without mandatory combat

Players may race for still-public opportunities and opt into Friendly Challenges, while ordinary Player Characters cannot damage, stun, knock back or trap one another.

**PASS.**

### Audience suitability

The baseline avoids compulsory unrestricted communication, gambling/staking and forced hostile loss. Platform-specific safety is still delegated to GDS-15.

**PASS.**

## 3. GDS-2 Lifecycle and Persistence Compatibility

### Session-Scoped social state

Party membership, leadership, pending invites, Pings and Friendly Challenges are transient social state rather than permanent progression.

**PASS.**

### Persistent outcomes remain exact

A Shared Objective may finalize a personal milestone/reward. Once finalized, Party churn, reconnect or leadership change cannot duplicate/revoke it.

**PASS.**

### Avatar failure

Reset/Recovery does not automatically dissolve Party membership because the Party relates to the player, not the temporary avatar.

**PASS.**

### Disconnect

A bounded Party Rejoin Grace may reserve a seat, but an absent player gains no raw-presence contribution. Leadership cannot remain frozen behind a disconnected leader.

**PASS.**

### Protected Load Failure

Social UI/state cannot authorize irreversible ownership/economy/progression actions while trusted persistent state is unavailable.

**PASS.**

## 4. GDS-3 Player Interaction and Onboarding Compatibility

### Input parity

Party, Ping, challenge, visitor and Shared Objective semantics are input-family independent. No keyboard/voice-only capability is required.

**PASS.**

### Active Context

Other players cannot physically or socially make a valid world interaction permanently inaccessible. Incoming social prompts cannot steal critical capture/transaction input focus.

**PASS.**

### Safe Arrival and Recovery

New/recovering players cannot be body-blocked at arrival points. Social status cannot bypass Safe Arrival or fabricate Recovery progress.

**PASS.**

### Onboarding protection

Onboarding-Protected Opportunities remain protected from unrelated players and Party members. GDS-10 adds no social race that can deny the learner's required first path.

**PASS.**

### Guidance versus progression

Pings/party waypoints guide players but do not themselves grant Landmark Discovery, Milestones or capture claims.

**PASS.**

## 5. GDS-4 Ownership and Collection Compatibility

### One ordinary owner

Party membership, leadership, Visitor status, Showcase viewing and Friendly Challenge outcomes do not create shared creature ownership.

**PASS.**

### Secured ownership safety

No social/PvP mechanic may involuntarily delete, transfer or Release a Secured Creature.

**PASS.**

### Creature Lock

Social interactions cannot remove or bypass another player's Creature Lock.

**PASS.**

### Discovery remains personal historical fact

Seeing a teammate's capture or another player's Vault does not grant Species/Mutation/Variant Discovery.

**PASS.**

### Provenance

Showcase/Party/visitor state does not rewrite creature Provenance.

**PASS.**

### Future transfer authority

GDS-10 creates no gifting/lending/trading shortcut. GDS-12 retains explicit ownership-transfer authority.

**PASS.**

## 6. GDS-5 Capture Compatibility

### One ordinary Engagement Claim

Public social competition exists before claim, but once a valid claim exists Party/friend/premium status cannot overwrite it.

**PASS.**

### Capture Attempt authority

An ordinary claim is not shared. Teammates cannot submit capture input as co-claimants.

**PASS.**

### Provisional Capture and Transport Custody

Capture Success still creates one carrier's custody. There is no Party handoff, escort transfer or interception mechanic.

**PASS.**

### Exact single-winner finalization

Party membership never duplicates one finite creature into multiple Secured Creatures.

**PASS.**

### Capacity and eligibility

Capacity/Capture Capability are evaluated per player. A stronger/richer teammate does not satisfy another player's capture prerequisites.

**PASS.**

### Interruption

Hazard/reset/disconnect semantics remain GDS-5 authority. Party membership cannot rescue a failed provisional outcome by transferring custody.

**PASS.**

## 7. GDS-6 Rarity and Variant Compatibility

### Social state does not alter odds

Party size, friend count, invites, visitors and social activity do not secretly modify Species/Mutation/Trait probability.

**PASS.**

### Variant Identity stability

Pings, claims, Party churn, observation, Friendly Challenges and visitor inspection do not reroll a creature.

**PASS.**

### Protected Variants

A rare/Protected Variant is still public until a valid claim, then protected by ordinary claim/custody rules. Party formation does not privately reserve it.

**PASS.**

### Discovery

Observation/showcase inspection does not create Mutation/Variant Discovery.

**PASS.**

### Status versus power

Displaying rare content provides social status without automatically increasing capture priority, Party reward or spawn power.

**PASS.**

## 8. GDS-7 Vault and Production Compatibility

### Visitor authority

GDS-10 retains read-only Visitor semantics and adds an owner-controlled audience policy without granting management authority.

**PASS.**

### Owner control

Visitors cannot claim Production Buffer, alter Production Assignments, spend Energy, release creatures or buy Vault upgrades.

**PASS.**

### Showcase identity

A displayed creature references the same Secured Creature; no duplicate ownership is created.

**PASS.**

### Production isolation

Party size, visitors and social activity do not automatically modify Passive Production, Production Slots, Buffer or Offline Window.

**PASS.**

### Capacity

Another player's Vault/Collection Capacity does not satisfy the owner's or Party member's capacity condition.

**PASS.**

## 9. GDS-8 Economy and Progression Compatibility

### Energy remains non-transferable

No Party wallet, member-to-member Energy payment or challenge wager exists.

**PASS.**

### Collaboration Rewards

Authorized Shared Objectives may create bounded personal active Energy rewards. These are game-originated Economy Sources and exact-once.

**PASS.**

### No raw-presence economy

Joining a Party, being friends, visiting a Vault, spectating or sending Pings does not mint Energy.

**PASS.**

### Active Milestones remain personal

Party play may help complete an authored Shared Objective, but passive/group presence cannot fabricate another player's discovery, Region Mastery or collection history.

**PASS.**

### Access Unlocks

Party membership does not bypass Energy/Milestone gates or buy another member's access.

**PASS.**

### Alt-account pressure

No direct Energy transfer plus contribution requirements prevent GDS-10 from becoming a simple alt-account wealth funnel.

**PASS.**

## 10. GDS-9 World Compatibility

### Public encounter model

Ordinary World Creatures remain public opportunities until a valid GDS-5 claim. A Party does not reserve a Habitat or spawn population.

**PASS.**

### Region Mastery

Landmark and Regional Collection components remain personal. Shared Field Objectives may grant personal valid completion only after personal Eligible Contribution.

**PASS.**

### Travel

Party leaders can suggest destinations but cannot force travel or bypass another player's Access Unlock/Travel Node state.

**PASS.**

### Acquisition-In-Progress

Party travel/social convenience does not bypass GDS-9 fast-travel prohibition during claim/capture/custody.

**PASS.**

### Rare encounters

Crowding does not alter Rare Encounter Stability or claim authority. Party size does not change rare odds.

**PASS.**

### Hazards

Social players cannot weaponize collision or direct combat to convert environmental hazards into player-caused involuntary value loss.

**PASS.**

### Safe infrastructure

Home Hub, Safe Outposts, Secure Points and Recovery Anchors remain non-trappable/non-body-blockable.

**PASS.**

**PASS.**

**PASS.**

### Narrow leader authority

Leader powers concern grouping only; they do not extend to player value/progression.

**PASS.**

### Deterministic succession

Leader departure/unavailability cannot freeze the Party.

**PASS.**

### Transient state

Party membership is not permanent progression and ordinary server departure ends it.

**PASS.**

## 15. Abuse and Griefing Audit

Covered design-level abuse classes include:

- Party Invite spam;
- challenge-request spam;
- Ping spam;
- leader kick-at-finish griefing;
- AFK Party reward leeching;
- alt-account collaboration farming;
- Party-size spawn/rarity manipulation;
- Party claim cycling to reserve public creatures;
- custody handoff/duplication attempts;
- body-blocking Safe Routes;
- blocking Secure Points/Recovery Anchors;
- player collision used to force hazards;
- visitor attempts to mutate Vault state;
- repeated visitor traffic as economy farm;
- social-status claim priority;
- paid social claim priority;
- Friendly Challenge wagering/value transfer;
- communication dependence on unrestricted chat/voice.

The specification defines a deterministic player-facing rule for each category.

**PASS.**

**PASS — authority preserved.**

### GDS-12 — Trading

No Party/friend/visitor/showcase mechanic transfers creature ownership or Energy. Trading remains explicit downstream authority.

**PASS — authority preserved.**

### GDS-13 — Monetization

**PASS — authority preserved.**

### GDS-14 — Presentation

GDS-10 requires legible consent, contribution, claim/custody, Ping and visitor states but does not prescribe final UI/VFX/audio.

**PASS — authority preserved.**

GDS-10 avoids requiring unrestricted communication and exposes privacy/invite control obligations, while leaving Roblox communication, reporting, blocking, age gating and moderation specifics to GDS-15.

GDS-10 identifies social metrics/experimentation guardrails but does not define referrals, persistent rankings, campaigns or retention cadence.

**PASS — authority preserved.**

**PASS — authority preserved.**

## 17. Product-Risk Review

### Risk: social play becomes mandatory

Mitigation: all ordinary core progression remains available solo; Shared Objectives may exist without replacing every personal objective.

**PASS.**

### Risk: Parties dominate scarce encounters

Mitigation: Party has no spawn reservation/rarity boost and ordinary claim semantics remain individual.

**PASS.**

### Risk: cooperation becomes alt-account economy farming

Mitigation: no reward for grouping/proximity, personal contribution required, exact-once bounded rewards, no direct Energy transfer.

**PASS.**

### Risk: multiplayer becomes hostile-loss PvP

Mitigation: no direct combat, custody theft, secured-creature theft, Energy theft, wagering or body-blocking.

**PASS.**

### Risk: social status fabricates progression

Mitigation: Showcases/status reference legitimate facts and observation grants no discovery.

**PASS.**

### Risk: unsafe communication is required

Mitigation: core coordination uses structured Pings and explicit UI state; platform chat/voice remains optional/downstream.

**PASS.**
