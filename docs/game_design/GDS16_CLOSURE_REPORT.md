## 4. Locked GDS-16 Decisions

### 4.1 Product success hierarchy remains authoritative

Comprehension/satisfaction outrank retention; retention outranks engagement; engagement outranks social value; social value outranks discovery/acquisition; monetization remains last.

### 4.2 No baseline login reward/streak/absence punishment

MonsterVault does not require attendance manipulation for retention.

### 4.3 Return Brief and Next Aspirations are guidance-only

They surface already-valid state/opportunities and cannot fabricate history, eligibility, odds or rewards.

### 4.4 Meaningful engagement is active product-value engagement

AFK, waiting, confusion and forced timers are not considered healthy engagement just because session length rises.

### 4.5 Discovery packaging must be honest

A click-through increase does not justify misrepresenting features, guarantees or conflict level.

### 4.6 No hidden behavioral/spend-based gameplay odds

Analytics/churn/spend/failure data cannot secretly personalize collectible scarcity, capture power, claim priority or event reward eligibility.

### 4.7 Experiments are governed

Decision-grade experiments require hypothesis, cohort, primary metric, guardrails, treatment/control, stable exposure, invariant review and stop/rollback conditions.

### 4.8 Shared public-value experiments use coherent shared rules

Players competing for the same shared opportunity are not placed under contradictory hidden rules.

### 4.9 Persistent-value experimentation is prospective and non-destructive

Existing legitimate Finalized Outcomes are not rerolled or revoked because an experiment ends.

### 4.10 Analytics is data-minimized

No unnecessary PII, sensitive-trait inference or raw chat content is required for retention optimization.

### 4.11 Safety/ownership/accessibility/fairness are Experiment Invariants

They cannot be toggled for KPI optimization.

### 4.12 Weak product health triggers redesign rather than stronger coercion

The GDS-1 pivot gate remains authoritative.
