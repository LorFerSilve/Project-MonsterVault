# GDS-13 Cross-System Validation

> **Phase:** GDS-13 — Monetization and Commercial Fairness  
> **Status:** PASS  
> **Purpose:** Validate GDS-13 against closed GDS-1 through GDS-12 authority and confirm clean downstream ownership boundaries.

## 1. Validation Scope

GDS-13 is cross-validated against:

- GDS-1 product identity, primary audience, moderate visible-but-non-coercive monetization and product-success hierarchy;
- GDS-2 persistent Finalized Outcomes, Active Presence and Protected Load Failure;
- GDS-3 modal input safety, onboarding and accessibility;
- GDS-4 ownership, Creature Lock, Release and collection persistence;
- GDS-5 capture/claim/custody/finalization;
- GDS-6 rarity, Mutation/Trait/Variant Identity, probability and provenance;
- GDS-7 Collection Capacity, Overflow-Held, Production Slots/Buffer and Offline Production Window;
- GDS-8 Energy, active milestones, progression gates and economy-health rules;
- GDS-9 world access, Region Mastery, Safe Routes and spawn integrity;
- GDS-10 social authority, competition and safety;
- GDS-11 Event Occurrences, contribution/rewards, event opportunity allocation and event timing;
- GDS-12 Trade Access, Creature Lock, Trade Cooldown, atomic transfer and Energy non-transferability;
- GDS-14 through GDS-16 downstream authority;
- Technical Architecture gating.
