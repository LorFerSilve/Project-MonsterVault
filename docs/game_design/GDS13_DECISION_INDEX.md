# GDS-13 Decision Index

> **Phase:** GDS-13 — Monetization and Commercial Fairness  
> **Status:** Accepted  
> **Purpose:** Phase-local record of strategic GDS-13 decisions and rationale. Detailed behavior remains authoritative in monetization/13_monetization_and_commercial_fairness.md.

## GDS13-D01 — Monetization Is Moderate, Deterministic, and Non-Coercive

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Baseline monetization prioritizes deterministic cosmetics/status and bounded durable convenience.

Payment is optional to the core collection/progression loop and cannot make ordinary free progression intentionally painful.

### Rationale

This matches GDS-1's commercial position while preserving player trust and collection value.

### Alternatives Rejected

- aggressive progression monetization;
- mandatory purchase gates;
- frustration-first storefront design;
- premium safety/accessibility.

---

## GDS13-D02 — Cosmetics and Status Are the Primary Paid Value

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Vault themes, decor, tool skins, presentation effects, status frames and similar deterministic presentation products are baseline-authorized.

They grant no gameplay authority, rarity identity, provenance or progression.

### Rationale

Presentation supports visible social value without changing finite gameplay opportunities.

---

## GDS13-D03 — Bounded Collection/Display Capacity Convenience Is Allowed

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

A durable paid entitlement may grant bounded additional Collection Capacity and/or Display Capacity.

It may not grant Production Slots, Production Buffer, Offline Production Window, production multipliers, extra Transport Custody or claim priority.

### Rationale

This realizes GDS-1's viable capacity-convenience direction without monetizing compounding production.

---

## GDS13-D04 — Unlimited Direct Paid Energy Is Not Authorized

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

There is no endlessly repeatable Robux-to-Energy baseline exchange.

A one-time Starter Value Bundle may contain a small deterministic Energy grant.

### Rationale

This permits bounded acceleration while preventing a soft-currency whale path from overwhelming GDS-8 pacing.

---

## GDS13-D05 — Production Progression Remains Non-Premium

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

No baseline paid:

- Production Slots;
- Production Buffer capacity;
- Offline Production Window extension;
- Passive Production multiplier.

### Rationale

Production compounds across time and would create durable payer/non-payer economic divergence.

---

## GDS13-D06 — No Paid Luck, Rerolls, Capture Power, or Claim Priority

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Payment cannot alter Species spawn odds, Mutation/Trait odds, Compound probability, current creature identity, capture success/difficulty, Engagement Claims or finite-opportunity priority.

### Rationale

Collectible scarcity and public opportunity competition must remain trustworthy.

---

## GDS13-D07 — No Randomized Paid Acquisition at Baseline

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

No paid loot boxes, gacha, eggs, crates, spins, random Creature Instances, random variants or randomized paid cosmetic containers are baseline-authorized.

### Rationale

Deterministic purchase value is easier to understand, safer for the target audience and avoids mixing commercial payment with collectible probability.

---

## GDS13-D08 — Core World, Event, and Trade Access Cannot Be Purchased

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Payment cannot directly grant Region Mastery, Landmark Discovery, Trade Access, Event Contribution, ordinary event entry or bypass active progression milestones.

### Rationale

Commercial acceleration must not replace active exploration, participation and ownership-system learning.

---

## GDS13-D09 — Starter Value Is One-Time and Bounded

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

The baseline Starter Value Bundle is one-time per account, deterministic, and may combine cosmetics with a small Energy/convenience grant.

It cannot skip several progression bands or fabricate active milestones.

### Rationale

This creates accessible starter value without becoming a permanent paid progression rail.

---

## GDS13-D10 — No Baseline Subscription or Server-Wide Gameplay Boost

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Recurring subscriptions and paid server-wide gameplay modifiers are not baseline-authorized.

Purely cosmetic server celebration may be considered if gameplay-neutral.

### Rationale

The launch commercial model remains simple and avoids recurring-functionality ransom or paid shared-world advantage.

---

## GDS13-D11 — Commercial Offers Must Be Truthful and Context-Safe

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

No fake countdowns, fake discounts, hidden price/content changes, loss-chasing paid rescue, repeated modal nagging or purchase prompts during critical acquisition/trade/recovery states.

### Rationale

Commercial conversion cannot override comprehension, consent or active gameplay safety.

---

## GDS13-D12 — Commercial Finalization Is Exact-Once and Reversal Is Non-Destructive

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Verified purchase grants finalize exactly once.

Commercial entitlement reversal may remove future entitlement benefit but cannot delete Secured Creatures or create Energy debt; paid-capacity loss uses safe GDS-7 reconciliation.

### Rationale

Commercial state must obey the same persistence/value-integrity standards as gameplay Finalized Outcomes.

---

## GDS13-D13 — Close GDS-13 Monetization and Commercial Fairness

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

GDS-13 is formally closed as **Complete — PASS**.
