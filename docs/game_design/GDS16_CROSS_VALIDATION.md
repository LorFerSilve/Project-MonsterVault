# GDS-16 Cross-System Validation

> **Phase:** GDS-16 — Retention, Discovery, Analytics, and Experimentation Boundaries  
> **Status:** PASS  
> **Purpose:** Validate GDS-16 against closed GDS-1 through GDS-15 authority and confirm that analytics/experimentation can improve the product without becoming a hidden override layer.

## 1. Validation Scope

GDS-16 is cross-validated against:

- GDS-1 product promise, session targets, success hierarchy, benchmark-relative gates and pivot criteria;
- GDS-2 persistence, exact-once outcomes, Recovery and Protected Load Failure;
- GDS-3 onboarding, Active Context and cross-device parity;
- GDS-4 ownership, collection history, Creature Lock and Overflow;
- GDS-5 capture claims, custody and finalization;
- GDS-6 rarity/Mutation/Trait/Variant identity and no hidden spending-based odds;
- GDS-7 Vault production/capacity/offline production;
- GDS-8 Energy, milestones, pacing and catch-up;
- GDS-9 world/mastery/spawn/travel/hazard rules;
- GDS-10 social value, Parties, Pings and non-destructive competition;
- GDS-11 event cadence, contribution/rewards and experimentable event parameters;
- GDS-12 trading safety/atomicity/cooldowns;
- GDS-13 monetization/fairness/commercial experimentation constraints;
- GDS-14 presentation/accessibility and commercial prompt hierarchy;
- GDS-15 platform/safety/privacy/eligibility constraints;
- GDS-17 final design-complete audit;
- Technical Architecture telemetry/config implementation.
