# GDS-16 Decision Index

> **Phase:** GDS-16 — Retention, Discovery, Analytics, and Experimentation Boundaries  
> **Status:** Accepted  
> **Purpose:** Phase-local record of strategic GDS-16 decisions and rationale. Detailed behavior remains authoritative in retention_analytics/16_retention_discovery_analytics_and_experimentation_boundaries.md.

## GDS16-D01 — Product Health Hierarchy Remains Above Metric Optimization

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

MonsterVault retains GDS-1's success hierarchy:

1. comprehension/satisfaction;
2. retention;
3. meaningful engagement;
4. intentional social value;
5. discovery/acquisition conversion;
6. monetization.

A lower-order metric win cannot override higher-order harm.

### Rationale

Retention/analytics should diagnose and improve the product, not redefine what "good" means after the fact.

---

## GDS16-D02 — No Baseline Login Reward, Login Streak, or Absence Penalty

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Baseline MonsterVault has:

- no daily login reward;
- no consecutive-day streak multiplier;
- no loss/reset for missing days;
- no mandatory daily/weekly checklist.

### Rationale

Return motivation should come from collection desire, visible progression, live opportunities and social stories rather than attendance coercion.

---

## GDS16-D03 — Return Brief and Next Aspirations Are Guidance, Not New Reward Authority

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Returning players may receive a concise Return Brief and progression-aware Next Aspirations based only on already-authorized game state.

These systems cannot grant missed rewards, change odds or fabricate eligibility.

### Rationale

Players need context after absence without a new retention economy.

---

## GDS16-D04 — Session Time Is Not Meaningful Engagement by Itself

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

AFK time, waiting, confusion and forced timers are not treated as meaningful engagement merely because session length rises.

### Rationale

The product promise is collection/progression/social satisfaction, not maximizing minutes at any cost.

---

## GDS16-D05 — Discovery Packaging Must Remain Truthful

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Icons, thumbnails, titles and descriptions may be tested, but cannot promise gameplay, guarantees, conflict intensity or collection outcomes that the actual experience does not deliver.

### Rationale

Click-through gains that increase bounce or damage trust are not healthy acquisition.

---

## GDS16-D06 — No Hidden Vulnerability, Spend, Failure, or Churn-Based Gameplay Odds

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Retention/purchase/churn models cannot secretly change per-player:

- Species odds;
- Mutation/Trait odds;
- capture power;
- claim priority;
- event reward eligibility;
- trade restrictions.

### Rationale

Analytics segmentation is not gameplay authority, and collectible scarcity must remain trustworthy.

---

## GDS16-D07 — Every Decision-Grade Experiment Requires Hypothesis, Primary Metric, Guardrails, and Stop Conditions

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Experiments require explicit:

- hypothesis;
- owning system;
- cohort;
- primary metric;
- guardrails;
- treatment/control;
- sample/duration context;
- invariant review;
- stop/rollback rule;
- result record.

### Rationale

Metric fishing and uncontrolled flags cannot become design authority.

---

## GDS16-D08 — Public/Shared Value Experiments Use Coherent Shared Context

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

An experiment that changes a public shared opportunity cannot give competing players secretly different rules for the same creature/event.

Where value-affecting tests are allowed, shared server/event context must be coherent.

### Rationale

This prevents hidden individualized scarcity/claim manipulation inside multiplayer competition.

---

## GDS16-D09 — Persistent-Value Experiments Are Prospective and Roll Back Non-Destructively

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Experiments may affect future eligible opportunities under owning-phase bounds, but may not reroll, confiscate or retroactively reprice legitimate Finalized Outcomes.

Rollback preserves valid treatment-earned value.

### Rationale

Experimentation cannot weaken the project's exact-once/value-trust contracts.

---

## GDS16-D10 — Analytics Uses Data Minimization and Excludes Raw Chat From Retention Optimization

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Product analytics does not require unnecessary personal information, sensitive-trait inference or raw chat/freeform message content.

Structured interaction types may be counted.

### Rationale

Retention optimization does not justify expanding privacy/moderation exposure.

---

## GDS16-D11 — Safety, Ownership, Accessibility, and Commercial Fairness Are Experiment Invariants

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

Experiment flags cannot alter foundational rules including:

- ownership integrity;
- Creature Lock;
- exact-once persistence;
- Protected Load Failure;
- trade atomicity;
- platform filtering/reporting eligibility;
- accessibility availability;
- no paid luck;
- no fake commercial urgency.

### Rationale

Some product contracts are safety/correctness properties, not variables.

---

## GDS16-D12 — Weak Core Product Triggers Redesign, Not Stronger Retention Pressure

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

GDS-1's pivot gate remains authoritative.

After repeated substantive iteration, weak comprehension, collection desire or retention is addressed through product redesign rather than:

- larger login bonuses;
- stronger FOMO;
- more notifications;
- longer forced sessions;
- more monetization pressure.

### Rationale

Retention mechanics cannot compensate for an unhealthy core loop.

---

## GDS16-D13 — Close GDS-16 Retention, Discovery, Analytics, and Experimentation Boundaries

**Status:** Accepted

### Decision

GDS-16 is formally closed as **Complete — PASS**.

Material changes to:

- no baseline login reward/streak/absence punishment;

- truthful discovery packaging;
- no hidden behavioral/spend-based odds personalization;
